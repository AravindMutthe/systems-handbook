# Synthesis after M — local draft, NOT pushed

Links, in order: A origins → B naming → C hardware →
D stack → E approval → F universal product → G control →
H interface → I leadership → J eras → K scheduler →
L employer / employee regulation → M radio broadcasting.

A need becomes a business, a business needs a name and
an address, a name needs hardware and a stack, hardware
needs approval, approval needs control, control needs an
interface people can use, an interface needs leadership
across eras, eras need scheduling, scheduling needs
regulation of work, and regulation of work meets
regulation of broadcast spectrum and media.

Feedback, as in Batch 4: outcomes do not end the story.
What is adopted creates a new need, and a new need
returns to A. This synthesis adds no new fact; it
links chapters already in the sequence.
