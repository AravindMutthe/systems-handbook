### [Added] explanation

Networks did not start with computers. They started with people
signalling to each other, then with mechanical signals, then with
the telegraph — and business needed them because a deal is only
as fast as the information behind it. That need is why organisations,
rules and coordination appear before any device in this sequence:
first the need, then the organisation that trades, then the rules
that govern that trading.

how it scales: names are delegated hierarchically, addresses are
allocated in blocks by region, and both are wrapped in regulation
and privacy rules. That is why naming and regulation belong in
one note, not in separate ones.
