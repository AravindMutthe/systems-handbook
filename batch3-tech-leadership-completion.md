### Era 3 — 2000 Internet / web / cloud Era [Added completion, core focus Original]
- Core business and focus: Building hypergrowth [Original,
  from hb-batch3-001: "Core focus = Building hypergrowth"]
- Org structure: [Added — proposed] distributed product /
  platform teams building web services, with central standards
  bodies above them — ICANN, IETF, W3C, as in Card J era ④.
  Central standards, distributed builders: the distribution
  era's "centralized team managing decentralization" grown
  into a platform + standards model.
- Tech strategy: [Added — grounded in Card J/H era ④] web
  servers, browsers + database, Apache, MySQL, open standards.
  The protocol work of Era 1–2 becomes web standards and
  web infrastructure.
- Leadership mindset: [Added — proposed] hypergrowth mindset:
  build for scale first, ship fast, grow the network before
  perfecting it. What matters is how many users, services,
  and connections the system can hold.
- Business model / strategy: [Added — grounded in Card J
  era ④] Ad network / subscription. The web pays by
  advertising and subscription, not by usage duration
  (telegraph) or accounting chargeback (mainframes).

### Era 4 — Mobile & cloud Era [Added]
Era name and lists from Card J era ⑤ and Card H mobile &
cloud era. Placed after web, before 5G, in his era order.
- Core business and focus: [Added — proposed] Building
  mobile-first, cloud-native, always-on services — the
  service follows the user, on a phone, on the cloud,
  not on a desk or in a corporate data centre.
- Org structure: [Added — proposed] small service teams,
  each owning a microservice, with a central platform team
  and central app-store / cloud IAM gates. Centralized
  platform, decentralized product teams — the same
  pattern as Era 2, now at product scale.
- Tech strategy: [Added — grounded in Card J/H era ⑤]
  APIs, microservices, mobile apps, cloud architecture
  (Kub + Terra), app stores, Cloud IAM.
- Leadership mindset: [Added — proposed] product mindset:
  user experience first, continuous delivery, ecosystem
  mindset — you do not own the whole stack, you ship
  into app stores and cloud platforms and live by their
  rules.
- Business model / strategy: [Added — grounded in Card J
  era ⑤] SaaS + usage. The service is rented by use,
  not licensed once (client-server) or sold by
  subscription alone (web).

### Era 5 — 5G & AI Era, 2020 – Now, till 2026 [Added]
Era name and "2020 – Now" from Card H (5G & modern,
2020 – Now) and Card J era ⑥ (5G & AI). "Till 2026"
is the owner's end point for this completion.
- Core business and focus: [Added — proposed] Building
  real-time, intelligent services — AI, edge, and 5G
  together, where the service does not just respond,
  it predicts, decides, and acts at the edge.
- Org structure: [Added — proposed] AI / platform teams,
  data + model teams, and edge teams, with central
  company rules and governance above them — Business
  controller (Private) (company rules), as in Card J.
  Central governance, distributed intelligence: the
  model runs at the edge, the rules stay central.
- Tech strategy: [Added — grounded in Card J/H era ⑥]
  AI models (GPT, Claude), GPU + IoT sensors + Edge
  nodes, AI pipeline MEC orchestration, (Pytorch,
  Tensorflow) service mesh.
- Leadership mindset: [Added — proposed] orchestration
  mindset: deciding what AI decides, what humans decide,
  and what the edge decides — control flow, not just
  data flow. The leader's job shifts from building
  links (Era 1) to managing decentralization (Era 2)
  to growing scale (Era 3) to shipping products
  (Era 4) to orchestrating intelligence (Era 5).
- Business model / strategy: [Added — grounded in Card J
  era ⑥] API Token. AI pays by token / API use, on top
  of SaaS + usage — the smallest unit of value yet,
  one token at a time.

- Telegraph: centralized, protocols, link state — establish communication.
- Distribution: centralized team managing decentralization, interoperability protocols — manage decentralization.
- Internet / web / cloud: distributed teams, web standards, hypergrowth — build hypergrowth.
- Mobile & cloud: small service teams, APIs / microservices / cloud, product mindset — build always-on services, SaaS + usage.
- 5G & AI, 2020 – 2026: AI / platform / edge teams, AI models / MEC orchestration, orchestration mindset — build real-time, intelligent services, API Token.
